Shipping Policy

At Perceptve, we strive to get your orders to you as quickly as possible.

Processing Time: Orders are typically processed within 2-4 weeks. Please note that processing time may vary depending on order volume and product availability.

Shipping: Once your order has been processed, it will be shipped via USPS with a standard shipping time of 5 business days. Please allow additional time for delivery during peak seasons or due to any unforeseen circumstances.

Tracking: You will receive a tracking number once your order has shipped, allowing you to monitor its progress.

Shipping Delays: Although we strive to ensure timely delivery, we cannot be held responsible for any delays caused by the postal service or other external factors.
